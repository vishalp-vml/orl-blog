Tips and tricks to improve the wheat farming and production.

Discover modern soil preparation techniques, optimal fertilization schedules, and tech-driven harvesting methodologies to multiply your wheat crop yield this season.

Dr. Sujay Agri-tech
Senior Agronomist, Swaraj Research
Published: Jan 24, 2026 | 8 min read

In This Article

  1. Soil Preparation & Seedbed Management
  2. Fertilizer Scheduling & Nutrient Contro
  3. High-Tech Mechanization With Swaraj Tractors

Key takeaway

Prepare a fine seedbed with disk harrows or rotavators before seeding. Aim for 3-5 cm depth using systematic seed drills.

"Modern mechanization is no longer optional for small to mid-scale farmers. Selecting high-yield seed variants coupled with the right tractor implement is the baseline to double produce.

1Soil Preparation & Seedbed Management

A high-yielding wheat crop begins with excellent soil conditions. Preparing a fine seedbed is critical to achieve uniform germination and growth. Using disk harrows or rotavators driven by a powerful Swaraj Tractor ensures deep soil aeration, effective organic material integration, and precise weed root termination before seeding.

Optimizing Seed Placement Depth

Seeds sown too deep struggle to push through, resulting in weak tillers. Seeds sown too shallow are prone to bird damage and dry surface conditions. The sweet spot is 3-5 cm depth using systematic seed drills.

2High-Tech Mechanization With Swaraj Tractors

Integrating modern machinery speeds up operations significantly, protecting the crop from sudden seasonal weather shifts. For instance, Swaraj's dual-clutch transmission allows seamless operation of high-performance rotavators and zero-till seed drills.

3Nutrient Management Protocols

PRO TIP

Focus on nitrogen at CRI, potash for stem strength, and organic compost for soil moisture retention.

Balanced nutrition forms the backbone of heavy crop heads. Keep these three core steps in mind during your seasonal scheduling:

  • Nitrogen top dressing at the critical crown root initiation (CRI) stage.
  • Sufficient Potash base application to build stem strength against heavy winds.
  • Organic compost mixture to maintain high soil moisture absorption capacity.

Stage Optimal Target Date Focus Action
Sowing Nov 1 - Nov 15 Seeding Drill
Tillering Dec 10 - Dec 20 First Irrigation
